# The tech landscape (what you’re actually using)

**OS = Operating System** — the software that runs your computer and lets apps run.

---

## Common operating systems

| Where you see it | What it is |
|------------------|------------|
| **Windows** | Most common on PCs and laptops (what you have). Runs Office, browsers, Cursor, games. |
| **macOS** | Apple laptops and desktops (MacBook, iMac). Common in design, some dev and research. |
| **Linux (e.g. Ubuntu)** | Free, open-source OS. Powers most servers, supercomputers, and a lot of cloud. Many data/science tools run best on Linux (you can try it later in a VM or dual-boot). |
| **Phones & tablets** | **Android** (Google, many brands) and **iOS** (Apple). Different from “desktop” but same ideas: apps, updates, privacy, app stores — all policy-relevant. |

---

## Why this matters for you

Policy debates touch “dominance” (e.g. Apple vs Google), privacy (OS and apps collecting data), and “digital divide” (who has what device and OS). Knowing the basics helps you read news and bills.
