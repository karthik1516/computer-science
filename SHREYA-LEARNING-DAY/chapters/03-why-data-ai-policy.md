# Why AI, data, and scripting matter in policy & public affairs

**Time:** ~10 min read

---

## In policy and law, people increasingly:

- **Use data** to see if a program works (education, health, crime), where inequality shows up, and how money is spent.

- **Use statistics** to interpret surveys, studies, and official numbers—exactly what you’ll do in policy and maybe law school.

- **Use scripts** (small programs) to clean data, combine datasets, and repeat analyses instead of copying numbers by hand.

- **Use AI** to draft memos, summarize long reports, or explore “what would the data say if we asked it this way?” (with human judgment always in charge).

---

## You don’t have to become a programmer

You do benefit from:

- Knowing what’s possible (data, scripts, AI).
- Being able to look at a dataset and a graph and ask good questions.
- Using tools (Cursor, spreadsheets, Python) to do one small analysis yourself.

---

## Why Python?

**Python** is a good first language: used in research, government, and data analysis; readable; and great for statistics and graphs. This lesson uses a little Python; you can also do a lot in **Google Sheets** (we mention both).
